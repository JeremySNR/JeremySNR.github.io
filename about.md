I'm Jeremy Smith. I write here about artificial intelligence, the infrastructure
it runs on, and who ends up owning that infrastructure.

Most of what I publish is about the gap between what governments and companies
say about AI and what they are actually building. I try to show my working, cite
the numbers, and take the strongest version of the opposing argument seriously
rather than waving it away.

I also build things. Most of my code is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/JeremySNR), largely small TypeScript primitives for
working with language models and some ongoing multi-agent research.

Everything here is written in a personal capacity and represents nobody's views
but my own.
