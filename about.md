I'm Jeremy Smith. I work as AI Innovation Partner at Travel Counsellors, and I
write here about artificial intelligence, the infrastructure it runs on, and the
question of who ends up owning that infrastructure.

Most of what I publish is about the gap between what governments and companies
say about AI and what they are actually building. I try to show my working, cite
the numbers, and take the strongest version of the opposing argument seriously
rather than waving it away.

Everything on this site is written in a personal capacity. The views are mine
alone and do not represent my employer.
